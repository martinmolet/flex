Questions

Before you commit.

Something else on your mind? Ask it live on the webinar or on a strategy call.

Which programme is right for me?
No units yet, or just one: Launch. Two or more, and you want to grow into a company: Scale. Not sure? Book the call and we’ll tell you honestly, even if the answer is neither.
Do I need to quit my job?
No. Launch is built to run alongside a full-time job: self-paced lessons plus live sessions in UK, US and Australian evenings.
Do I need to own property?
No. We cover the ways to run units without buying them, the agreements that protect you, and when buying starts to make sense.
Does it work outside the UK?
Yes. It’s taught in English for the UK, US, Canada, Australia and beyond. Local rules differ, so each module flags what to check where you live.
What does it cost?
Launch is X and Scale is Y. The webinar, the strategy call and the checklist are free.
Do I need to use Base360?
It’s the platform we built to run The Flex, so the programme is taught on it. The playbook and systems work whatever tools you use.
